## Contribution Statement

Youssef Hussein, JangHyeon Lee, and Oishee Bintey Hoque contributed **equally** to the ARFA project and submission. The student team jointly contributed to the system conception and design, development and integration of the agentic and geospatial workflow, implementation and testing of flood-response capabilities, experimental/demo evaluation, preparation of figures and demonstrations, and writing and revision of the submission materials.

The work was carried out collaboratively, with the three student members sharing responsibility for the technical development and final competition submission. No student team member is designated as having a greater contribution than the others.

Dalton Lunga served as the **ORNL mentor**, providing research guidance, technical feedback, domain perspective, and review throughout the project. His role was mentorship and research supervision; the three Ph.D. students are the student participants responsible for the competition submission.

We confirm that this statement accurately represents the student status and contribution structure of the ARFA team for the ACM SIGSPATIAL OASIS 2026 Challenge submission.
